SnapScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

SnapScan (also known as 随手扫 / 隨手掃 / サッとスキャン / 찰칵스캔) is a document scanner and PDF/Word converter. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

Your documents stay on your phone

Photos you take or import, scanned pages, and the PDF and Word files you convert are processed entirely on your device. We do not upload, collect, store or share them. Exported files are saved only where you choose (for example Documents/SnapScan or Pictures/SnapScan) or sent only to the app you pick when sharing.

Camera

The camera permission is used only to photograph documents inside the app. Images are not sent anywhere.

No account, no ads, no tracking

SnapScan has no sign-in, shows no ads, and does not use the advertising ID or any analytics service of its own.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Full version" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own the purchase so it can unlock features on your devices; this check happens between the app and Google Play and is not stored on our servers.

Google services

The optional "Google scanner" feature uses Google's ML Kit document scanner provided by Google Play services. Scanning happens on your device, but Google may collect diagnostic information (such as device model, app version, performance and error data) to keep the service working. Google Play Billing is likewise provided by Google. Their handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Free usage counter

To offer 5 free scans and 3 free conversions, the app keeps a small counter on your device. It is not sent anywhere and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

SnapScan is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: cellsense.help@gmail.com

随手扫 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

随手扫（SnapScan）是一款文档扫描和 PDF / Word 转换应用。本政策说明你使用本应用时数据如何处理。

你的文件只留在手机上

你拍摄或导入的照片、扫描页面，以及转换的 PDF 和 Word 文件，全部在你的设备上处理。我们不会上传、收集、保存或分享它们。导出的文件只保存在你选择的位置（例如「文档/随手扫」「图片/随手扫」），分享时只发送给你选择的应用。

相机

相机权限只用于在应用内拍摄文档，照片不会发送到任何地方。

无账号、无广告、无追踪

随手扫无需登录，不显示广告，不使用广告 ID，也没有接入任何自有的统计分析服务。

购买

可选的「完整版」一次性购买由 Google Play 处理，我们看不到你的付款信息。应用会向 Google Play 查询你是否已购买，以便在你的设备上解锁功能；这一查询只发生在应用和 Google Play 之间，不会保存在我们的服务器上。

Google 服务

可选的「谷歌扫描」功能使用 Google Play 服务提供的 ML Kit 文档扫描器。扫描在你的设备上完成，但 Google 可能会收集诊断信息（如设备型号、应用版本、性能和错误数据）以维持服务运行。Google Play 结算同样由 Google 提供。相关数据处理请参见 Google 隐私权政策。

免费次数记录

为了提供 5 次免费扫描和 3 次免费转换，应用会在你的设备上保存一个计数，不会发送到任何地方，卸载应用或清除数据后即被删除。

儿童

随手扫并非面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集任何个人信息。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，我们会更新此页面及上方日期。如有疑问请联系：cellsense.help@gmail.com
